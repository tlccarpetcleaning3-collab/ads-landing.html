Professional Area Rug Cleaning in Danville, IL

Area rugs collect dirt, dust, allergens, and spills that regular vacuuming can't fully remove. Over time, even the best-looking rug is hiding grime deep in its fibers.

On-Site Area Rug Cleaning

At TLC Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning, we bring our professional equipment directly to your home and clean your area rugs right where they are. No need to roll them up or haul them anywhere - we handle everything on-site with the same professional-grade truck-mounted equipment we use for carpet cleaning.

Our Area Rug Cleaning Process:

  1. Inspection - We check the rug's fiber type, construction, and condition to determine the best cleaning approach
  2. Pre-treatment - Problem areas and stains get targeted treatment with professional solutions
  3. Deep Cleaning - Using our truck-mounted system and TMF Swivel Wand, we extract deep-set dirt, allergens, and odors
  4. Stain Treatment - Stubborn spots get individual attention until they're gone
  5. Final Inspection - We walk through the results with you to make sure you're satisfied

Why Professional Rug Cleaning?

Store-bought rug cleaners and rental machines don't have the power to truly deep clean an area rug. Our truck-mounted system generates the heat and suction needed to pull dirt from deep within the fibers - leaving your rug cleaner, fresher, and lasting longer.
