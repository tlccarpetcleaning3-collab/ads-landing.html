Why do pet stains keep coming back after cleaning?

Store-bought products only treat the surface. Pet urine soaks into the carpet pad and subfloor. Professional enzyme treatment reaches the full depth of the stain.

Can you remove old pet stains?

Yes - even stains that are months or years old can be treated. We use UV light to find every stain, including ones you can't see.

Will professional treatment stop my pet from going back to the same spot?

In most cases yes. Pets return to spots they can smell. Once the uric acid is fully broken down, the scent trigger is eliminated.

UV Light Pet Stain Detection

At TLC Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning, we use professional UV light technology to find every pet stain - even the ones you can't see. This means nothing gets missed, and we can treat the actual source of the odor instead of just covering it up.

Professional Enzyme Treatment

Store-bought pet stain removers mask the smell temporarily. We use professional-grade enzyme-based treatments that break down the uric acid crystals in pet urine at a molecular level. This doesn't just cover the odor - it eliminates it permanently.

  1. UV Light Inspection - We scan your carpets to find every stain, visible or hidden
  2. Pre-treatment - Enzyme solution is applied directly to each stain and given time to break down the urine crystals
  3. Deep Extraction - Our truck-mounted system extracts the broken-down stain material from deep within the carpet and pad
  4. Deodorizer Application - Professional-grade deodorizer neutralizes any remaining odor
  5. Follow-up - We check every treated area to confirm the stain and odor are gone
