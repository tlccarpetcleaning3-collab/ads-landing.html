Spring Cleaning Checklist for Danville IL Homeowners

By TLC Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning | March 2026

Illinois winters are tough on homes. Between tracked-in salt, mud, and months of sealed-up indoor air, spring is the time to reset. Here’s a room-by-room checklist from our team—plus the stuff most people forget.

Why Spring Cleaning Matters More Than You Think

After a Danville winter, your home has been collecting allergens, dust, and grit for months. Opening windows helps, but deep cleaning is what actually removes the buildup. Spring cleaning isn’t just about tidiness—it’s about air quality, carpet longevity, and starting the season fresh.

Your Room-by-Room Checklist

🟉 Living Room & Family Room

  • Vacuum all carpet, including under furniture and along baseboards
  • Spot-treat any stains from winter (salt tracks, coffee spills, pet accidents)
  • Clean upholstered furniture—couches trap dust, pet hair, and body oils all winter
  • Wipe down ceiling fans (they collect dust all season while running on low)
  • Wash throw blankets and pillow covers
  • Schedule professional carpet cleaning to remove deep-set winter grime

🍳 Kitchen

  • Deep clean tile and grout (especially around the stove and sink)
  • Pull out the fridge and clean behind and under it
  • Scrub the inside of the oven and microwave
  • Wipe down all cabinet fronts—grease builds up over winter cooking season
  • Clean and organize the pantry

🛁 Bathrooms

  • Scrub tile and grout on floors and in showers
  • Clean exhaust fans (they pull moisture and collect dust all winter)
  • Wash or replace shower curtains and bath mats
  • Check caulk and grout lines for mildew—address early before it spreads

🛏️ Bedrooms

  • Flip or rotate mattresses
  • Wash all bedding including mattress protectors
  • Vacuum under beds (dust bunnies thrive here)
  • Clean carpet in closets—rarely vacuumed but collects fiber and dust

🚪 Entryways & Hallways

  • Deep clean entryway carpet or area rugs—these take the worst beating
  • Clean or replace doormats
  • Wipe down door handles, light switches, and handrails
  • Address salt stains on carpet or tile before they set permanently

The 3 Things Most People Skip

  1. Upholstery cleaning. Your couch sees more use than almost any surface in your home, but most people never clean it. A professional upholstery cleaning removes allergens, odors, and years of body oils that make fabric look dingy.
  2. Area rug cleaning. Area rugs trap dirt on both sides—the top collects foot traffic, and the bottom traps grit against your floor. Vacuuming only addresses half the problem.
  3. Grout restoration. Kitchen and bathroom grout absorbs moisture and stains all winter. A professional cleaning can make grout lines look new again without the backbreaking scrubbing.

When to Call a Professional

DIY spring cleaning handles surfaces, but some jobs need professional equipment:

  • Carpet cleaning: Truck-mounted hot water extraction reaches deep fibers that vacuums and rental machines miss. Our truck mount runs at higher temperatures and stronger suction than anything you can rent at the store.
  • Pet stain & odor removal: If you’ve had pet accidents over the winter, surface cleaning won’t reach urine that has soaked into the pad. We use specialized enzymes and extraction to treat the source.
  • Tile & grout: Professional equipment restores grout to its original color without damaging the surface.
  • Upholstery: Fabric couches and chairs need gentle but thorough cleaning that won’t damage the material or leave it waterlogged.
