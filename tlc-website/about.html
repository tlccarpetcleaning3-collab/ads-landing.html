About TLC Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning

TLC Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning has been serving the Danville, Illinois community since 1991. What started as a small family business has grown into Vermilion County's most trusted name in professional carpet cleaning - and we're still family-owned and operated.

Our Story

For over 35 years, we've built our reputation one clean carpet at a time. We don't cut corners, we don't use gimmicks, and we don't leave until the job is done right. That's why generations of Danville families have trusted us with their homes.

Our Team

We're a family business in every sense of the word. When you call TLC, you're not getting a franchise or a call center - you're getting real people who live and work right here in Vermilion County.

Our Commitment

  • We sanitize our equipment every day and between every customer
  • We use truck-mounted equipment for the deepest possible clean
  • We never use shampoo - only hot water extraction that leaves zero residue
  • We stand behind every job - if you're not happy, we'll make it right
