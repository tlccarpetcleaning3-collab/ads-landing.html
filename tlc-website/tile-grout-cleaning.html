Can dirty grout be cleaned or does it need to be replaced?

In most cases, professional cleaning restores grout to near-original condition. Replacement is only needed if grout is cracked or crumbling.

How often should tile and grout be professionally cleaned?

Every 1-2 years for most homes. High-traffic areas like kitchen floors may benefit from annual cleaning.

Does vinegar clean grout?

Vinegar provides mild surface cleaning but can actually erode grout over time, making it more porous and harder to keep clean. Professional alkaline cleaners are safer and more effective.

Why Professional Tile Cleaning?

The porous surface of grout absorbs dirt, grease, and bacteria over time. Regular mopping can't reach what's embedded below the surface. Our professional equipment and solutions penetrate deep into the grout to pull out what home methods leave behind.
