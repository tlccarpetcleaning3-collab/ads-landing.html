# How Often Should You Clean Your Carpets? A Danville IL Guide

*By TLC Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning | March 2026*

If you're like most homeowners in Danville, IL, you probably vacuum regularly but wonder — how often do you actually need professional carpet cleaning? The answer depends on your household, but here's the bottom line: **at least once a year, and more often if you have kids, pets, or allergies.**

## The General Rule

The Carpet and Rug Institute recommends professional hot water extraction cleaning every **12 to 18 months** for the average household. But "average" doesn't describe most homes we visit in Vermilion County.

## Your Household Determines Your Schedule

**Every 6 months:**
- Homes with pets (dogs, cats, or both)
- Households with young children
- Anyone with allergies or asthma
- Smokers in the home

**Every 12 months:**
- Couples without pets or kids
- Homes with low foot traffic
- People who remove shoes at the door

**Every 3-4 months:**
- Multiple pets
- White or light-colored carpet
- High-traffic commercial spaces

## Why Vacuuming Isn't Enough

Your vacuum picks up surface dirt — about 85% of what's sitting on top. But the other 15%? That's ground deep into the carpet fibers where your vacuum can't reach. Over time, this includes:

- **Dust mites** (a single square yard of carpet can hold up to 100,000 dust mites)
- **Pet dander and urine** (even if you can't see it)
- **Bacteria and allergens** from shoes, skin cells, and outdoor pollutants
- **Sand and grit** that acts like sandpaper, wearing down carpet fibers

This is why carpets in high-traffic areas look worn and matted even when they're "clean."

## The Hidden Cost of Waiting Too Long

Here's something most people don't realize: **skipping professional cleaning actually shortens your carpet's life.** That built-up grit grinds against fibers every time you walk on it, breaking them down permanently. 

Most carpet manufacturers require professional cleaning every 12-18 months to **keep your warranty valid.** Skip it, and you could void your warranty without even knowing.

New carpet costs $3-8 per square foot installed. Professional cleaning costs a fraction of that and can add years to your carpet's life.

## What We Recommend for Danville Homes

After 35 years of cleaning carpets in Vermilion County, here's what we tell our customers:

1. **Vacuum high-traffic areas twice a week** (hallways, living rooms, entryways)
2. **Address spills immediately** — blot, don't rub
3. **Use doormats** at every entrance
4. **Schedule professional cleaning once a year minimum** — twice if you have pets
